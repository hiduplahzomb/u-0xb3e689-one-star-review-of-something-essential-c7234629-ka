A deadpan one-star review of the sun, filed in earnest against a thing
that cannot be fixed.

> One star. The sun has no off switch, no dimmer, and burns everything it touches — and customer service has ignored my complaints for 4.5 billion years.

`main.py` writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`) and verifies it byte-for-byte.
